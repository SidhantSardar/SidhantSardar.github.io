## Problem

A technical platform has to make sense to risk and compliance leaders, who are not engineers, in the first few seconds.

## Key decisions

1. **Problems before features.** Four risk cards come first, so the reader recognises their situation.
2. **A gated executive guide** as the main lead capture.
3. **Same design language as the consultancy site**, so the two brands support each other.
