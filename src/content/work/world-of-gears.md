## Problem

ESG and human-rights due diligence is complex. Buyers need to see quickly that this team does the work on the ground, not just on paper.

## Key decisions

1. **Lead with the buyer's pain.** The page names five problems the buyer already feels before it talks about services.
2. **Proof above the fold:** 17 countries, 100+ implementations.
3. **Toolbox as a CMS library**, ready to grow into a lead magnet.
